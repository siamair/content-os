# คลังคำศัพท์ (Modifiers) เรียงตาม AVB-6

## 1. Subject — ทำให้ตัวแบบชัด
- ท่าทาง: sitting upright, mid-stride, leaning forward, looking over shoulder, paws tucked
- ลักษณะ: weathered hands, freckled skin, glossy coat, matted fur, crisp tailoring
- การกระทำ: reaching for, pouring, laughing mid-sentence, yawning
- สัตว์: breed/mix, coat pattern (tortoiseshell, calico, tabby), coat length, ear tufts, ruff, plumed tail, eye color

## 2. Setting
- ภายใน: cluttered home office, sunlit kitchen, minimalist studio, rustic café
- ภายนอก: rain-slick street, misty pine forest, rooftop at dusk, tropical beach
- ชั้นความลึก: foreground blur element, midground subject, background bokeh

## 3. Vantage
- ระยะ: extreme close-up, close-up, medium, full body, wide establishing
- มุม: eye-level, low angle, high angle, top-down/flat lay, Dutch tilt, over-the-shoulder
- เลนส์: 24mm wide, 35mm, 50mm, 85mm portrait, 100mm macro, 200mm telephoto compression
- รูรับแสง: f/1.4 dreamy bokeh, f/2.8, f/8 everything sharp

## 5. Mood
- สงบ: serene, cozy, nostalgic, tender
- พลัง: energetic, triumphant, playful
- เข้ม: moody, mysterious, melancholic, tense
- หรู: regal, elegant, exclusive

## 6. Style
- ภาพถ่าย: photorealistic, documentary, editorial, film photography (Portra 400, Ektar, Tri-X)
- ภาพวาด: watercolor, oil painting, gouache, ink wash, pencil sketch
- 3D: clay render, isometric, octane-like render, low-poly
- พื้นผิว: film grain, halftone, paper texture, risograph

no text, no watermark, no logo, no extra limbs, no distorted anatomy, no plastic skin, no CGI look, no oversaturation
